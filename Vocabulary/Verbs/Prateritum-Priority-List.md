# Präteritum Priority List — B1 Exam and Speaking

এই list-টি সব German verb-এর list নয়। Spoken German-এ completed past-এর জন্য সাধারণত Perfekt ব্যবহার করা হবে; এই verbs-গুলোর Präteritum আলাদাভাবে activeভাবে শেখা হবে কারণ এগুলো কথায়, reading-এ বা B1 writing-এ খুব গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।

## Tier 1 — must actively produce

| Infinitiv | Meaning | Präteritum | Perfekt | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sein | হওয়া | war | ist gewesen | কথায় খুব common |
| haben | থাকা/আছে | hatte | hat gehabt | কথায় খুব common |
| werden | হওয়া/হয়ে যাওয়া | wurde | ist geworden | auxiliary + common verb |
| können | পারা | konnte | hat gekonnt | modal |
| müssen | করতে হওয়া | musste | hat gemusst | modal |
| wollen | চাওয়া | wollte | hat gewollt | modal |
| sollen | উচিত হওয়া | sollte | hat gesollt | modal |
| dürfen | অনুমতি থাকা | durfte | hat gedurft | modal |
| mögen | পছন্দ করা | mochte | hat gemocht | modal |

## Learning policy

- Tier 1: full conjugation and active sentence production.
- Tier 2: full form practice after Tier 1; use in narration.
- Tier 3: first recognize in reading, then produce when needed.
- Regular verbs such as `machen → machte`, `lernen → lernte`, `arbeiten → arbeitete` will be generated from the regular pattern rather than memorized as isolated irregular forms.
- All other verbs: learn Präsens and Perfekt first; keep the Präteritum as a reference form until it becomes high priority.

## Current status

Tier 1 modal verbs and `sein`/`haben` have been practiced. The remaining list is not yet complete.
