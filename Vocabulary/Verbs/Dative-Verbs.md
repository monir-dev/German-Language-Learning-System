# Core Dativ Verbs

These verbs take a Dativ person/object. Learn the verb together with its case and an example.

## B1 core roadmap: 20 useful Dativ verbs

There is no single official total for “B1 Dativ verbs”. This is a practical high-frequency roadmap, not an exhaustive dictionary list.

### Batch 1 — already introduced

`helfen, danken, antworten, gefallen, gehören, fehlen, zuhören, vertrauen`

### Batch 2 — next priority

`folgen, begegnen, gratulieren, glauben, passen, schmecken, wehtun, gelingen`

### Batch 3 — later B1 expansion

`verzeihen, widersprechen, ähneln, leidtun`

Many ordinary verbs can also have a Dativ recipient plus an Akkusativ object, for example `Ich gebe dem Mann das Buch.` Those are tracked separately from fixed/core Dativ verbs.

| Verb | Meaning | Dativ example | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| helfen | সাহায্য করা | `Ich helfe dir.` | Practiced |
| danken | ধন্যবাদ দেওয়া | `Ich danke ihr.` | Practiced |
| antworten | উত্তর দেওয়া | `Ich antworte ihm.` | Needs revision |
| gefallen | পছন্দ হওয়া | `Das gefällt mir.` | Practiced |
| gehören | কার/অধিকারভুক্ত হওয়া | `Das gehört uns.` | Practiced |
| fehlen | অভাব হওয়া | `Du fehlst mir.` | Practiced |
| zuhören | মন দিয়ে শোনা | `Ich höre euch zu.` | Practiced |
| vertrauen | বিশ্বাস করা | `Ich vertraue Ihnen.` | Practiced |

## Pronoun reminder

`mir, dir, ihm, ihr, uns, euch, ihnen/Ihnen`

- `ihm` = him/it, masculine or neuter singular
- `ihr` = her, feminine singular
- `Ihnen` = you, formal
